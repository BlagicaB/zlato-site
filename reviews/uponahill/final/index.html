Mon · Sept 21 Announcement · Price drop Facebook + Instagram

The final show — and a night for our Teachers

Facebook

One more night on the hill — and we're saving something special for it. September 30 is the final Up On A Hill of the year, with Liam Kazar: a longtime member of Jeff Tweedy's touring band, and a songwriter whose records sound like a Sunday and a Saturday night at the same time. It's also our night for Teachers, hosted by the New Buffalo Community Education Foundation. More than 400 people came out to the first two shows, which is more than we hoped for in a first year. So for the last one we've brought the price down to make it easy to bring someone with you — general admission is $25, reserved seating is $50. Gates at 4. DJ Quin Kirchner on records at 4:30. Music at 6. Apple Blossom Resort, New Buffalo. Every ticket supports Neighbor by Neighbor, Dune Coast and the New Buffalo Community Education Foundation. uponahill.org
